The corrected deterministic bundle is designed to emit one mechanically derived `ready` Map ticket after executing stages 1–3 only. Compilation itself emits `candidate`; `compiler/readiness.py:evaluate` is the sole final-lifecycle writer and consumes verified creation-gate receipts. Stages 4–7 remain pending and no Map or production Engine change is made.

Closed Draft 2020-12 schemas cover every normative nested structure. Per-run timestamps live only in bounded `gate-receipts/`; stable ticket identity excludes them. Each rerun replaces those paths with a newly executed attempt receipt set.
