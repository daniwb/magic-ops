# Factory NG contract review v1

This directory stores the frozen high-impact governance review package and the
independent Sol/Opus review receipts.  The package is generated by
`scripts/factory-ng-contract-review-pack.py`; do not edit generated manifests,
review packs, or raw reviewer output by hand.

Acceptance requires both reviewers to return `REVIEW_DECISION: ACCEPT` against
the same `package_sha256`, plus package/hash validation. Until then the reviewed
contracts remain proposed and have no production effect.

Review 1 was rejected by both reviewers. Its frozen package and raw receipts
are retained as `review-1-*`; the remediation adds executable counterexamples
for unsupported model blame, missing dual high-impact approval, and child
profile authority override before Review 2 is requested.

Review 2 was rejected by both reviewers. It found two remaining bypasses: an
unparented non-baseline profile could assert authority/budget, and a single
`sol-opus` reviewer profile could satisfy both high-impact approvals. Review 3
adds schema and loader enforcement plus executable counterexamples for both.

Review 3 was rejected by both reviewers. Sol found that an `accepted`
execution receipt could omit the harness gate, patch, attempt, and raw-counter
evidence described by the contract. Opus additionally found that a child
profile could smuggle write/shell/network authority through a free-form adapter
block. Its raw receipts are retained as `review-3-*`. Review 4 strengthens the
receipt schema and makes V1 adapters explicitly registered and read-only.

Review 4 was rejected by Sol: named Sol and Opus entries could reuse the same
review artifact. Review 5 adds distinct immutable review and reviewer-attempt
identities plus a relationship validator and duplicate-artifact counterexample.

Review 5 was rejected by Sol because the classification label itself could
downgrade token/cost or acceptance work. Review 6 records affected Factory
contract areas and mechanically elevates the four high-impact areas before
review evidence is evaluated.

Review 6 was rejected by Sol because the 500k warning/reflection threshold was
not represented in accepted receipts. Review 7 records a normalized,
non-double-counted calculation and requires a linked reflection ticket above
the warning threshold without cancelling otherwise valid work.

Review 7 was rejected by Sol because aggregate telemetry was not reconciled to
per-call raw counters. Review 8 adds aggregate reconciliation and explicit
unavailability propagation before effective-token budgeting is evaluated.

Review 8 was rejected by Sol because reviewer profile-name patterns were not
proof of reviewer identity. Review 9 binds each review digest to a
harness-written review receipt checked against the closed Sol/Opus registry.

Review 9 was rejected by Sol because authenticated reviews were not required
to cover the same current package. Review 10 binds every accepted review
receipt to the Factory change's authoritative reviewed-package digest.

Review 10 was rejected by Sol because the closed Sol/Opus registry was applied
to all work. Review 11 limits that registry and package-match rule to
`change_class: factory`, while proving a normal Qwen-reviewed refactor still
validates through ordinary evidence and gate policy.

Review 11 was rejected by Sol because accepted provenance omitted several
fields required by the prose. Review 12 makes roots, result commit, telemetry,
changed paths, and before/after scope mandatory for accepted outcomes.

Review 12 was rejected by Sol because cache/reasoning unknowns could still be
collapsed to zero. Review 13 reconciles all token-counter aggregates, not only
input and output.
